## Joyce's *Elements*

The textual narrative, diagrams, proposition layout, page structure,
and accompanying images under
[`content/elements/`](content/elements/) &mdash; the Lektor source
for Euclid's *Elements*, Books I&ndash;XIII &mdash; are
**&copy; David E. Joyce**, used by his permission.

Dr. Joyce wrote these between 1996 and 2020 as part of his online
edition of the *Elements*, originally hosted at Clark University. His
permission to republish this content (and to port the underlying
applet to TypeScript as `geomlib`) was granted to Nelson Brown in
May 2026. For the exact wording of his permission grant, see the
[NOTICE](https://github.com/brownnrl/euclid/blob/main/NOTICE.md) in
the `geomlib` source repository.

**Third parties wishing to redistribute Dr. Joyce's narrative content
should obtain separate permission from him.** The permission granted
to Nelson Brown was specific; it is not transitive.

The companion repository
[`euclids-elements.org`](https://github.com/brownnrl/euclids-elements.org)
holds the same Joyce content in its original hand-authored HTML form,
plus the Compass Geometry, round / inversive, and Euler line tutorials
&mdash; all under the same Joyce-by-permission terms.
